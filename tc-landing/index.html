100% Compatible

Transcendent Campus plugs into your real Infinite Campus account, so your the app accurately displays your grades. All you have to do is log in with Google, and the app will take care of the rest.

iOS Native

Unlike the official app, Transcendent Campus was designed with iOS in mind. It's design is consistent with the rest of iOS, and is written in native Swift and SwiftUI, while the official app is just a wrapper on top of the web app. This also enables Transcendent Campus to offer better integrated features.

Widgets + Siri

Check your grades at a glance with simple widgets that show your grades on your home screen. Ask Siri about tomrrow's schedule, or the grade you got on that test. Such obviously good ideas that I have no idea why they didn't put them in the original app.

Social

Transcendent Campus adds social features you wish Infinite Camus had, like checking what classes your friends are in, or sharing your grades and advice on a test.
